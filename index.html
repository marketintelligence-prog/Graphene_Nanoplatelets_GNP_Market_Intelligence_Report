Product Overview & Identity

Introduction

Graphene nanoplatelets (GNP) are short stacks of graphene sheets — typically 3–40 layers, 1–15 nm thick and 0.5–25 µm across. They sit between single-layer graphene and bulk graphite and are the commercial “workhorse” of the graphene family because they can be made at tonne scale from natural graphite. At 0.5–5 wt% they raise stiffness, barrier, thermal and electrical conductivity of polymers, coatings, concrete and battery electrodes. More than 700 commercial graphene forms exist [5], so GNP is bought by specification (layers, lateral size, surface area, carbon %), not by name alone.

Strengths

  • Existing import, sourcing and lab-supply network
  • India feedstock (flake graphite) and partners (Tata, AdNano, TACC)
  • Low duty (BCD 2.5%)
  • CRO arm can add characterisation (Raman, BET, TEM)

Weaknesses

  • No own process IP
  • Many grades → inventory and QC load
  • Customers need application support we must build

Opportunities

  • Battery localisation after China graphite controls
  • Masterbatch route lowers customer barrier
  • Kerala/IICG ecosystem
  • Coatings and pipe makers seeking performance claims

Threats

  • Chinese price floor
  • Indian producers integrating forward
  • Analyst CAGRs overstate real demand
  • Substitution by carbon black / CNT
